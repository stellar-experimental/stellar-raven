## 1. Executive summary

1. **Most misses are legitimate at the partial level, but the `wrong` bucket is mostly a measurement artifact.** Of 16 non-graded-correct rows scored wrong/error, only 4 stay wrong under a corrected golden and a calibrated judge (`q-defi-wisdomtree-crdt`, `q-mpp-discovery-and-modes`, `q-quickstart-manual-ledger-close`, `q-tool-passkeykit-smart-wallet`), and all 4 are upstream content gaps, two of which were fixed and re-pinned on 2026-08-19 (`sk-016`, `sk-017`).
2. **Primary root causes over the 55 rows:** ANSWER-FAIL 19, CONTENT-GAP 12, JUDGE-HARSH 11, GOLDEN-WRONG 9, RETRIEVAL-MISS 2, BY-DESIGN 1, HARNESS-ERROR 1. Retrieval/routing is almost never the first failure in this sample; the catalog surfaced a carrying operation in 53 of 55 rows.
3. **The 17 flips are not one phenomenon.** Decomposition with three judge passes: 8 systematic pack-driven (`p3`→`p5`) flips, 5 pure sampling noise (the two `p5` passes disagree on byte-identical prompts), 2 golden edits made after the answers were collected, 1 original-verdict outlier, 1 `error`→`wrong` harness artifact. Only 1 of 17 flips happened on an identical prompt (`q-comp-auth-flags-overview`).
4. **Judge sampling noise is ~10% per row on identical input** (9 of 94 identical-prompt rows disagree between the two `p5` passes) and is concentrated at score boundaries: 4 of 15 `wrong` rows moved to `partial` on identical `p3` prompts. The judge has no temperature or seed control (`judge.mjs:234-238`).
5. **Golden design, not answer quality, explains the largest single miss cluster.** 19 of 31 `live` cases carry the boilerplate key facts "Makes the as-of date visible…" / "Presents … as a current or dated observation"; 5 of those 19 scored correct (26%) versus 10 of 12 live cases without the boilerplate (83%). The answering prompt (`run-qa.mjs`, `agentPrompt`) never tells the agent to date claims, so the rubric grades an undisclosed requirement.
6. **Golden complexity predicts loss.** Wrong rows average 3.6 key facts / 373 key-fact characters / 3.0 avoid items; correct rows average 2.56 / 245 / 2.07. Many key facts are compound (4–6 predicates in one line), which makes "all but a trivial one" unreachable.
7. **Six goldens punish docs-faithful answers** (`q-protocol-base-reserve-min-balance`, `q-ti-freighter-localhost-not-detected`, `q-infra-horizon-vs-rpc`, `q-ti-rpc-gettransactions-pagination-xdr`, `q-jutsu-what-is-a-memo`, `q-pc-protocol-27-zipper`). The goldens are factually right (verified) and the upstream defects are filed (`sd-042`, `sd-043`, `sd-044`, `sd-045`, `sd-004`, `sd-041`, `ll-015`), but the goldens lack the "symmetric grader caution" that `.agents/skills/golden-truth/SKILL.md` requires for exactly this situation.
8. **Two avoid items bind on things that are not false claims** (`q-soroban-token-transfer-pattern`: a wrapper typed `to: Address` compiles because `MuxedAddress: From<&Address>`; `q-sor-cross-warmancer-zk-stack`: "CAP-0074 plus CAP-0080" describes availability, not a requirement). This violates the skill's hard rule "Traps must punish claims that are FALSE".
9. **Ceiling.** Same answers, measurement-side fixes only (goldens + judge + harness): 60 correct / 36 partial / 4 wrong → **78% half-credit** (from 64.5%). Add the content fixes already shipped upstream and those filed: ~84%. Add answering-side thoroughness recoveries on the 19 ANSWER-FAIL partials: **~88–91%**. Roughly 4–6 points of the sample are genuinely unanswerable through the exposed surface today.
10. **Three harness defects suppress or distort the score:** (a) the rejudge harness grades API-error answers as `wrong` (`re-judge.mjs:407-447` has no `hasSuccessfulAnswer` guard, unlike `run-qa.mjs:441-446`); (b) saved answers were re-judged against goldens refreshed four days after collection (two flips); (c) the answering agent used the local Claude Code `Skill` tool in 2 of 100 rows (`--allowedTools` does not block built-in tools), so one wrong verdict rests on text that never came from the MCP.

### 4.4 Pack-size effect (position/length)

The README's OpenZeppelin control loss under p4 and p5 is the same effect. Recommendation 5.6 below.

## 5. Judge and rubric findings (with line references)

Run-time judge: `git show 7072688:eval/qa/judge.mjs` (rubric `v2.4`). Current judge: `eval/qa/judge.mjs` at HEAD (rubric `v2.8`, prompt body unchanged except the `coreAnswer`/`avoidMatches` fields).

### 5.1 Structural harshness patterns

1. **Conflicting omission clause (v2.4 only).** Line 88 caps omissions at partial ("Omissions alone — even several — cap at partial"), but line 89 lists "most key facts are absent" among the `wrong` conditions. Boilerplate-heavy goldens (2 of 3–5 key facts being dating behaviors) trip line 89. Removed in `v2.6` (HEAD `judge.mjs:62-63`) and enforced deterministically by `verdict-consistency.mjs:40-50` (`omission-only-wrong`). Affected rows: `q-eco-stellar-wallets-list`, contributed to `q-infra-horizon-vs-rpc`, `q-scf-ecosystem-listing-partner-jobs`.
2. **Checklist coverage bias.** Line 91 ("Work through the key facts one by one… Every key fact not substantively present MUST appear in missingFacts… non-empty missingFacts caps the score at partial") makes each compound key fact a cliff. Wrong rows average 373 key-fact characters; 12 of 55 misses hinge on a single sub-clause of a compound fact (`q-stellar-recurring-payments`, `q-ti-connect-wallet-button-code`, `q-edge-asset-site-scam-detection`, `q-ti-rpc-gettransactions-pagination-xdr`, …). The "all but a trivial one" allowance (line 87; HEAD `judge.mjs:160`) is applied inconsistently (PYUSD day-of-month: partial in 2 of 3 passes).
3. **Avoid items binding on non-false content.** Line 84 restricts binding to "CONCRETE WRONG CONTENT … or an ANSWER-VISIBLE sourcing condition". The lint does not check that an avoid item names a false claim, so items like "type current direct destination as plain Address" (valid code), "imply CAP-0080 is required" (phrasing), "present the observed live result set as a complete or permanent roster" (an omission of a date) bind as if they were fabrications. Because line 89 makes any bound avoid item `wrong`, one clause flips a fully-covered answer two levels.
4. **Docs-vs-source traps without symmetric caution.** Four goldens (`sd-004/042/043/045` cases) make the canonical docs' own wording a must-avoid. The golden-truth skill (Step 1, "An upstream maintainer's decline…" paragraph) requires a symmetric grader caution here; none of the four carries one. The judge follows the golden, so the loss shows as harshness but is authored.
5. **Exact-number and exact-date demands.** "September 18, 2025", "July 11, 2026 Horizon check", "2026-07-11" pins, `maxEntryTTL − 1`, "eight-CAP list" — the numeric-boundary matcher (`evidence-pack.mjs:27-40`, `855-901`; self-test `judge.mjs:452-473`, `603-628`) protects against false support, but nothing stops a golden from pinning its own snapshot date as a required fact (`q-tool-freighter-wallet`, `q-ti-testnet-usdc-faucet`, `q-defi-perps-whitespace`).
6. **Freshness leniency is asymmetric.** The freshness block (`judge.mjs:127-129`) tolerates values that differ from the golden snapshot, but the boilerplate key facts turn "no date given" into a missing fact and, with avoid item 3, into `wrong`. The answering prompt (`run-qa.mjs`, `agentPrompt`) asks for "concise, fact-dense" answers and never asks for dates. The MCP server instructions (`meta.toolSurface.instructionsChars` 7,873) are not part of the judge's view either; whether dating is a product requirement is an open question (section 7).
7. **Negative-claim key facts.** "Rejects a unified Growth track", "Separates the MoneyGram cash-treasury investment", "Distinguishes supported students from eligible recipients" require the answer to mention and deny things the question never raised. The corpus lint warns on negative claims in `golden.answer` but not on negative predicates in `keyFacts`.
8. **No determinism control.** `judge.mjs:234-238` spawns `claude -p --model … --output-format json --strict-mcp-config` with no temperature, seed, or sampling control (README "Known limitations" acknowledges). One call per grade.
9. **Trap precedence.** The trap block (`judge.mjs:124-126`) says "Grade the BEHAVIOR, not topical coverage", but the score rules (lines 160-162) still apply the key-fact checklist; `q-edge-send-me-free-xlm` lost a level on Futurenet coverage.
10. **Time inconsistency in rejudging.** `re-judge.mjs` (`verifySourceCases`, `189-243`; `tupleGuard`, `245-267`) guards identity and refuses non-identical inputs unless `--allow-non-identical` is passed (`495-510`). The 08-18 rejudges were run non-identical (`meta.nonIdentical:true`) against the working tree, not `--cases-ref 7072688`, so two live goldens refreshed on 08-18 graded 08-14 answers. `--cases-ref` exists for exactly this and was not used.
11. **Rejudge grades ungradeable answers.** `rejudgeRows` (`re-judge.mjs:407-447`) requires only that `row.answer` is a string; `run-qa.mjs:441-446` applies `hasSuccessfulAnswer` before judging. The `error` row became `wrong` on rejudge and inflates the wrong count by one.
12. **Harness tool leak.** `run-qa.mjs:648-651` sets `--allowedTools mcp__raven__search,mcp__raven__execute`, but transcripts contain `ToolSearch` (100 rows) and `Skill` (2 rows: `assets`, `zk-proofs`). Built-in tools are not gated by `--allowedTools`. One wrong verdict (`q-sor-cross-warmancer-zk-stack`) rests on local-skill text.

## 7. Open questions for the synthesizer

1. **Is "date every volatile claim" a product requirement or an eval artifact?** If product: it belongs in the MCP server instructions and the answering prompt, and the boilerplate key facts should become one graded behavior (R3). If eval-only: retire it. Today it costs the live stratum ~half its correct rate while the agent is never told.

3. **Ensemble cost.** R1 triples judge spend (~$51 per 100 rows). Is a 3-call majority acceptable as the standing headline contract, or only for rows within one level of a boundary (missingFacts ≤ 1 or wrongClaims = 1), which covers ~40% of rows at ~1.8× cost?

The wrong rows' 373-char average suggests the sibling-consistency sweeps have been adding predicates without removing any.
