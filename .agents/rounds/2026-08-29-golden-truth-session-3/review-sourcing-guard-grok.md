## Should the warning class stay advisory?

Yes. Keep `sourcing-guard` as a warning, not an error.

Most flagged items here are useful answer-visible guards for volatile numbers, disputed attributions, and unsupported labels. An answer that cites a dated primary source should not be graded wrong. Turning the class into a hard fail would push authors to delete those guards or to hide them in notes, which is the opposite of the owner’s evidence-first instruction.

Remove only demonstrated cruft:

1. Reword traps whose “without evidence” clause lets a still-false claim through once any citation is attached (oracle “permanently”; Kelp “actively maintained”).
2. Reword regex false positives on otherwise-good concrete false claims (`lacks ingestion support` on the Go ingest case).
3. Do not mass-delete the remaining warnings to lower the count.
