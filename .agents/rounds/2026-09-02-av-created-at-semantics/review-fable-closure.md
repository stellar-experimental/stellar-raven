## F1 — Medium. The tutorial score sentences contradict each other and name the wrong mechanism.

- Location: `.agents/rounds/2026-09-02-av-created-at-semantics.md`, the three sentences
  starting "Fable measured the old false sentence at rank four with a score of 242."
- Evidence: the ledger says "Evidence-true video wording scored 222 against the rank-five
  threshold of 216. It stayed below the top five because rank four scored 219 and tie/order
  rules applied." A score of 222 is above both 219 and 216. No tie rule can place it below them.
  I re-scored the question through `searchCatalogPage` with tier output:

  | Wording | A/V score | Tier | Rank |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | `main` | 274 | gated | 4 |
  | old first sentence, new returns text | 242 | gated | 4 |
  | final | 212 | backfill | 30 |
  | final plus one evidence-true `video` | 222 | backfill | 28 |

  The real mechanism is the tier seam. With the final wording the entry no longer passes the
  vendor token-coverage gate for this question (`src/catalog/scoring.ts:56-60`, under 60 percent
  coverage). It enters as a tier-2 backfill hit. A backfill hit outranks a gated hit only when its
  score is at least `TIER_INTERLEAVE_MARGIN` (1.6) times the gated score
  (`src/catalog/search.ts:183-189`). Against the rank-5 gated score of 216 that needs 346. Both
  222 and 212 fall far short. The old false sentence lifted the entry over the coverage gate; the
  evidence-true wording does not, and no evidence-true single word can.
- My closure review carried the same error. Its sentence "still below the rank-5 score of 216
  plus the rank-4 score of 219" was wrong and is superseded by this section. The conclusion of
  that review stands: the accepted movement is justified, and only the false sentence restores
  rank 4.
- Consequence: a decision record that a future reader will test against the scorer and find
  false, which invites re-opening the accepted trade.
- Smallest repair: replace the three sentences with: "Fable measured the old false sentence at
  rank four (score 242, gated tier). The final wording drops the entry below the vendor
  token-coverage gate for this question, so it becomes a tier-2 backfill hit (score 212; 222 with
  one extra evidence-true `video`). A backfill hit needs 1.6 times the rank-five gated score
  (216, so 346) to enter the top five. No evidence-true wording reaches that."
