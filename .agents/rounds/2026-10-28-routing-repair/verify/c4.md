## Verdict

1. **"T1 passes."** T1 fails on main, the committed catalog. It passes only on fresh.

## Findings

**F1. T1 still fails on main. The fill cannot fix it.** I traced the controlled-vocabulary query on
c4-main:

- The gated page from `diversifyByService` already holds three Lumenloop entries:
  `search_directory` 335, `get_project` 329, and `search_content_semantic` 267.
- The gated pool has no candidate from another service for that slot, so the soft quota overflows
  before any fill runs.
- The full-page backfill holds only `lumenloop.get_categories`. That is the same service, so the fill
  has nothing to defer to.
- `preserveIntentWithinServiceQuota` then swaps one Lumenloop entry for another. The count stays at
  three.

The cause is the same as in my C1 finding T1: the C1 scoring hunk shrinks the gated pool. On fresh,
Scout has more gated entries, so T1 passes there. The decision from `c1.md` is still open. Is the
two-per-service cap a contract? If it is, no fill-side change satisfies it on main.

**F2. The full-page block is outside the ruling and causes every new graded loss.** C4 adds
`if (gatedPageIsFull) selected = fillPageWithinServiceQuota([], [...selected, ...backfill], limit)`.
On a full page, this step lets an admitted backfill entry displace an over-quota gated entry. It does
not apply the `TIER_INTERLEAVE_MARGIN` test that `preserveStrongBackfill` applies (`candidate.score >=
1.6 × victim.score`). The ruling says: "No other quota or selector change (no 'admit under-quota
services on full pages')." This block is that change. Measured from `c4n` to `c4`:

- Losses: ecosystem-listing cardHit5 (both sources), blend-repo cardHit5, sdf-marketing-grant
  cardHit5, and trusted-asset-list cardHit5 (fresh).
- Diagnostics: extended cardHit5 falls from 17 to 16 (main) and from 17 to 15 (fresh). Schema-word
  gated hits fall from 78 to 76.
- Gains: x402 top3 (both sources) and x402 top5 (fresh).

Recommendation: remove the block. x402 is the check-6 tier-margin shape (`c1.md`, R1). The ruling
says not to add a mechanism for it in C4.

**F3. The short-page fill is not narrow. It changes the effective quota from 6 to 2.** The C2 rescue
was `diversifyByService(ungated, limit + 10, …)`. The quota inside that call is `serviceQuota(15)` =
ceil(15 × 0.4) = 6. On a five-slot page, 6 means no quota. So short pages and all-backfill pages were
in pure score order. C4 counts quota 2 and includes the gated entries already on the page. Effects
from `c4p` to `c4n`:

- **231 rows change page order on fresh, and 232 on main.**
- Graded results: **11–12 gains and 0 losses.** Friendbot, Reflector, the OZ workspace holdout, and
  SAC-or-SEP-41 all gain.
- **All of the D2 growth:** 7 new hackathon rows and 3 new locator rows. The RWA probe gets one more
  capture.
- Three more unit failures. One of them is "keeps long technical all-backfill advice broad-only and
  ranking untouched". That test name states the pure-order contract that this change breaks.

The fill has no score floor and no admission test for the replacement entry. On a short page, "an
admitted candidate" means the whole ungated pool, and any token overlap admits an entry. Example
(fresh, `q-ti-connect-wallet-button-code`, an all-backfill page): Docs 752, 700, and 662 leave the
page. `stellar-ecosystem-scout` 628, `stellar-dev.dapp` 616, and `scout.getHackathonSubmission` 611
take their places. Every new D2 row has this shape: a weak hackathon or locator entry fills the
third service slot.

This is the same interference class that C6 targets. Recommendations for the lead:

1. Decide if the "limit + 10" effective quota on short pages was a contract. The test
   names suggest it was.
2. If the quota is wanted, do not add a score floor, because that is a tuned constant. Re-measure the
   `c4n` short-page fill on top of C6. With C6, the intent gate can reject the hackathon and locator
   entries before they reach the fill. `c4n` is the best partial candidate on graded rows:
   2 / 3 losses (OZ and Groth16, plus fresh x402), extended cardHit5 17 on both sources, and
   legacy cardHit5 113 on both sources. It fails D2.

**F4. E1 is correctly scoped. Over-stemming brings back one stopword fragment.** I compared all
681 queries (the corpus plus the probes) with the 48 keyword-bearing entries:

- Scores: 358 of 65,376 scores change. All 358 go up. No score changes to or from null. Three pages
  change order, and no grade changes.
- Porter newly allows 284 distinct pairs (query token, keyword). It removes none, so it is a superset
  of the C2 rule on this data.
- Most pairs are inflections: `connected`/`connect`, `verified`/`verify`, and `deployed`/`deploy`.
  Some are derivational: `authorization`/`author`, `product`/`production`, `general`/`generate`,
  `customer`/`custom`, and `validator`/`valid`. I accept these as the cost of a standard stemmer.
- The defect: `stemmer("one")` = `on`. The keyword `one` appears on `scout.analyzeEcosystem` and
  `scout.getLeaderboard`. So the stopword `on` is now "supported" on those two entries. Its substring
  hits in the augmented description count again. This is the class that C2 removed. It causes 334
  pair instances and **77 of the 358 score increases**. For example, `getLeaderboard` ungated on
  "Which wallets run on Stellar?" goes from 41 to 43. `id`/`ide` has the same shape (14 instances).
- Effect today: small, with no grade change. Fix: in the "allowed" test, skip query tokens in
  `STOPWORDS` and tokens shorter than 3 characters. This uses only the existing list and adds no
  new list.

The evidence is in `porter-scope.mjs`, `porter-diffs.mjs`, and `runs/c4-porter-scope-main.json`.

**F5. E2 and the vendored code.** The values are identical, and the math is unchanged. Two notes:

- E2 edits the vendored `search-scoring.ts`. It exports `FIELD_WEIGHTS` and extracts
  `coverageBonus`. The header already says "adapted", so this is allowed. But it adds structural
  distance from upstream `@cloudflare/codemode`. The alternative is a pin test with no vendor edit.
  The lead decides.
- The new `vendor/porter.ts` header names `https://github.com/words/stemmer` with no version or
  commit. I could not check the copy byte for byte offline, because the package is not installed.
  Recommend: pin the version in the header.

**G1. Generality.** The diff has no operation ID, service name, or query string. The only new
constant is the cache cap of 5,000, which is not a scoring constant. No test, case, label, gate,
golden, or override file changes. CONFIRMED general.
