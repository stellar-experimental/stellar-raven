### The null thresholds T (amendment A4; recomputed, not assumed)

Model, as loop 1 used it: each of n rows flips with probability p, up or down with equal chance, independently; T is the smallest net (downs minus ups) at or above the 95th percentile.
The function reproduces loop 1's table exactly (p 0.34, n 50 gives 7 / 8 and P(net at least 6) 9.1%; 0.20 gives 5 / 6 and 4.0%; 0.14 gives 4 / 5 and 1.9%; `scans/p0/tnull.mjs`).
**P0's measured all-rows rate p = 6 / 46 = 0.13** (8 / 50 = 0.16 with the exact rows left in). No drift, so p = 0.34 is not used.

| n (comparison) | **T95 at p = 0.13 (measured)** | T97.5 | T95 at p = 0.16 (exact rows in) | T95 at p = 0.34 (loop 1's gross rate) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 58 (P1 or P2 against P0, all rows) | **4** | 5 | 5 | 7 |
| 50 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 7 |
| 46 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 6 |
| 34 (a tier-matched subset, size as in the arm 1 pair) | **3** | 4 | 4 | 6 |
| 30 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 |

Per A4, T is recomputed per comparison from its own n and this rate; report the tier-matched net and its T beside the all-rows net, and a result that reaches T only on all rows is "no decision".
**Two cautions the orchestrator should weigh before fixing T:** (1) the rate rests on 6 flips, so its interval is wide (p between about 0.06 and 0.26 at 95%); (2) T = 4 is below the 6 or 7 loop 1 used. P1
differs from P0 by a map removal that reaches every row, so its flips will include real moves, and a threshold of 4 is easy to reach. I report T = 4 as the measurement and do not change the rule; p = 0.16 gives 5 and the loop 1 rate gives 7.
