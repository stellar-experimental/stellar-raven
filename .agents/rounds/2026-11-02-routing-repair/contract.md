## Decision B (taken)

The twelve acceptance checks of TODO "Preserve structured routing intent across extraction caps and
gate tiers" mix two kinds of test:

- **Mechanism checks** test a general property of the scorer: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. They stay required.
  A scorer that lets `has` match inside `phase`, or lets `through` route alone, is wrong for every
  query, not only the probe.
- **Named-row checks** pin single rows or single ranks: 7 (eight rows), 9 (two gains), 10's Groth16
  rank, 11 (one taxonomy operation), and the strict reading of 12. Five designs on 2026-10-28 failed
  only on such rows, while they fixed real mechanism defects. Under the owner's principle these
  become diagnostics inside one aggregate rule (below), not vetoes.

Check 8 (RWA exposure) leaves this repair. `GET /api/rwa` stays excluded; issue #167 keeps its own
controls and its `it.fails` markers. The repair must not change RWA exposure.

## Acceptance rule for a candidate (all must hold, on BOTH sources: current main sources and the
frozen fresh source)

A1. Mechanism checks 1–6 pass.

A2. Aggregate non-regression. Let a graded row be any row of the 544-row routing corpus that has a
    grade in the legacy, skills, holdout, or extended lanes. Count graded wins W and graded losses L
    against the unchanged-scorer baseline on the same source. Require W − L ≥ 0, and require
    that no single gate metric (each top1/top3/top5/cardHit5 of legacy, skills, holdout, extended)
    falls by more than 1 from the baseline.

A3. Every graded loss has a written cause from the per-row trace. A loss is acceptable only when the
    cause is (a) removed spurious evidence (a substring, stopword, prefix, or schema-only match that
    the mechanism checks forbid) or (b) a different entry that now ranks higher on legitimate
    evidence for the same question. A loss caused by new spurious evidence fails the candidate.

A4. Interference does not grow. Non-hackathon top-five captures by hackathon operations, and
    captures by `locateSource` where exposed, do not increase over the baseline on the same source
    (the 2026-10-28 D2 guard, as counts).

A5. Fresh-source absorb. On the fresh source the candidate has no graded loss that the unchanged
    scorer does not also have on that source (the 2026-10-28 D1 losses-only reading of check 12),
    and `reviewSubmission` stays excluded.

A6. No operation-specific or question-specific exception, no new `SKILL_DESCRIPTION_OVERRIDES`
    entry, no label, expected answer, or floor change to hide a loss. Floors in `eval/gates.json`
    move only in the shipping change, only to the measured values of the accepted candidate, and
    only within A2.

A7. Runtime: `npm run typecheck`, `npm test`, `npm run test:smoke`, and `npm run build` pass after
    the expected generated artifacts are regenerated by their scripts. Any test that encodes a
    named-row expectation changed by an accepted A3 loss is updated with that cause, never deleted.
    A runtime model call on the search path (for example a query embedding) must have a bounded
    timeout and a deterministic lexical fallback, and must keep `globalOutbound` null in the sandbox.

## Choosing among passing candidates

Prefer, in order: the larger W − L summed over both sources; then fewer A3 losses; then the smaller
runtime cost (no network call on the search path beats one); then the smaller diff.

If no candidate passes, nothing ships, the floors stay, and the TODO records the measured results.

## Amendment 2 (2026-11-02, after round 1; adopted before round 2 was built)

Timing record: Amendment 1 has SHA-256 657ca2ed…494b. Round 1 (lanes rt-a and rt-b) measured six
designs, and none passed. rt-b reported three internal conflicts of the rule. Each change below
rests on a property of the rule or of the baseline that holds without any candidate number. No
floor, threshold, or loss rule is relaxed.

- H1 check 4 becomes a diagnostic. Its positive (the account-merge query keeps
  `search_docs_in_category`) holds on the unchanged scorer only because the stopword `i` matches the
  prefix of `in` in that entry's id and name. The 2026-10-09 TODO history already recorded this.
  G10 forbids that evidence, so check 4 and G10 cannot both hold for a scorer without new evidence.
  G2 and G3 still count the graded rows of that query.
- H2 G10 scope: G10 forbids (i) a query token of length 1–2 or a stopword that matches by prefix or
  substring, and (ii) a prefix or substring pair that is not a morphological variant (`in`/
  `integration`, `1`/`lvl1`, `with`/`without`, `has`/`phase`, `approach`/`app`). A plural fold or a
  prefix pair that the existing `tokensOverlap` rule accepts (shorter ≥ 4 characters, ratio ≥ 0.75)
  is morphology, not this defect. `type`/`types` is therefore out of G10's scope. The gates note
  that cites `type` names a source-text item (a Returns field name inside a description).
- H3 check 5 stays required as a whole. Its `has`/`phase` half is the substring defect. Its
  `status phase value` half is schema-only admission. A design must fix both.
- H4 an admission rule for open-world retrieval entries (full-text, semantic, research, or speech
  search) is allowed only when it keys on a manifest property that applies to every entry of that
  class, and `ARCHITECTURE.md` describes it. An operation-id list is an A6 exception. G7 and G11
  apply to such a rule unchanged.
- H5 every other rule of v1 and Amendment 1 is unchanged, including G2, G3, G4, G5, G7, and G13.
